# A small toki pona <-> english corpus.  Articles are separated by a '----'
# line. Articles containing only lines that start with '#' are ignored.
# Otherwise the '#' prefix is used for the optional opening title line. Within
# an article each sentence is split in two lines. The first line is a toki pona
# sentence, and the second line is its english translation. Sentences are
# seperated by a blank line.  English vocabulary can be used within the toki
# pona sentences by enclosing them is square brakets, and vice versa. (this
# file is 7-bit ASCII only!)

----

ni li nasin pona lili:
This is a simple example:

toki pona la, ante toki pi [hungry] li ni: (wile moku).
In toki pona, the translation of "hungry" is "want to eat".

----

mi pilin pona.
I feel good.

----

sina wile moku.
You want to eat.

----

jan li toki.
A person speaks.

----

mi kama sona.
I learn.

----

suno li suli.
The sun is big.

----

kili li pona.
Fruit is good.

----

mi lukin e tomo.
I see a building.

----

sina moku e kili.
You eat fruit.

----

jan lili li musi.
Children play.

----

mi wile lape.
I want to sleep.

[bigotry] li ni:
[bigotry] is this:

jan li lukin ike e jan ante tan ni: jan ante li sama ala ona.
A person judges other people because those people are not the same as them.

ona li wile ala sona e jan ante.
They do not want to know other people.

ona li wile ala kama sona tan jan ante.
They do not want to learn from other people.

tan ni la ona li pana e pilin ike tawa jan ante.
Because of this, they give bad feelings to other people.

ni li pona ala. ni li pakala.
This is not good. This is harmful.

ni ale li [bigotry].
All of this is [bigotry].

[flying monkey] li jan pona lukin pi jan ike, taso ona li pali e wile pi jan ike.
A [flying monkey] is someone who looks like a friend of the abusive person, yet carries out that person's wishes.

[narcissistic abuse] la jan ike li wile lawa e ale, li wile ala kute e pilin ante.
In [narcissistic abuse], the harmful person wants to control everything and does not want to hear other feelings.

jan ike li toki wawa tawa [flying monkey] kepeken musi, pi mi mute, anu pakala.
The abuser speaks strongly to the [flying monkey] using flattery, shared secrets, or threats.

tan ni la [flying monkey] li lon lawa pi jan ike, li pali sama waso lete lon sewi.
Because of this the [flying monkey] moves under the abuser's control and strikes from a distance.

ona li toki e ijo ike tawa jan ante, li pali e pakala tawa nimi pi jan ike.
They spread harmful messages to other people and carry out damage in the abuser's name.

jan ante li pilin pakala tan ni la kulupu li pakala kepeken wawa sin.
Other people feel hurt, so the community breaks further with new force.

jan sona li ken weka e wile pi [flying monkey] kepeken sona, nasin lawa, en pilin pona pi jan ante.
Knowledgeable helpers can remove the [flying monkey]'s mission through education, boundaries, and empathy for others.

ni ale li [flying monkey] lon ma pi [narcissistic abuse].
All of this is a [flying monkey] within the world of [narcissistic abuse].

# Plot summary / retelling of the original "Star Wars" trilogy (1977-1983) by George Lucas.

toki pi ma [Star Wars] open la ma lawa [Galactic Empire] li utala wawa tawa kulupu pi [Rebel Alliance].
At the start of the Star Wars saga, the Galactic Empire fights fiercely against the Rebel Alliance.

jan [Princess Leia] li awen ala e lipu wawa pi ilo moli [Death Star], tan ni la ona li pana e lipu ni tawa ilo [R2-D2] kepeken wile alasa e kama sona.
Princess Leia refuses to surrender the powerful Death Star plans, so she passes them to the droid R2-D2 to protect the chance for knowledge.

ilo [R2-D2] en [C-3PO] li tawa ma lete pi suno [Tatooine] en li kama ken tawa jan lili [Luke Skywalker].
R2-D2 and C-3PO travel to the desert world Tatooine and eventually reach the young Luke Skywalker.

jan suli [Obi-Wan Kenobi] li kama sona e [Luke] en li toki e lipu pi mama ona pi nasin [Jedi].
Obi-Wan Kenobi finds Luke and reveals the legacy of his Jedi family.

[Obi-Wan] li wile e ni: [Luke] o kama [Jedi] sin, o kepeken ilo mun [lightsaber], o awen e jan [Leia].
Obi-Wan urges Luke to become a new Jedi, wield his lightsaber, and help rescue Leia.

[Luke], jan lawa [Han Solo], soweli [Chewbacca], en jan ilo li weka tan [Tatooine] lon ilo [Millennium Falcon] tawa alasa e [Leia].
Luke, Captain Han Solo, Chewbacca, and the droids depart Tatooine on the Millennium Falcon to rescue Leia.

kulupu ni li jo e [Leia] lon ilo [Death Star] en li kama ante tawa ike pi jan lawa [Darth Vader].
The group reaches the Death Star, frees Leia, and faces the terror of Darth Vader.

[Obi-Wan] li utala lon nasin ilo en li moli tan ni la jan ante li ken weka.
Obi-Wan duels within the station and sacrifices himself so the others can escape.

kulupu pi jan wawa li tawa kulupu [Rebel] lon ma mun [Yavin] en li lon e wile moli pi [Death Star].
The heroes reach the Rebel base on Yavin and prepare to destroy the Death Star.

[Luke] li tawa mun lon ilo lili en kepeken kon pi [Force] tawa pakala e ilo moli ni.
Luke flies his starfighter and uses the Force to trigger the shot that destroys the battle station.

tenpo suno pini la kulupu [Rebel] li len lon ma lete [Hoth], taso ma lawa [Empire] li alasa e ona kepeken ilo wawa suli.
Later the Rebels hide on the icy planet Hoth, but the Empire hunts them with massive war machines.

[Luke] li tawa ma kasi [Dagobah] tawa kama sona tan jan suli [Yoda] lon nasin pi kon [Force].
Luke travels to the swampy world Dagobah to learn from Jedi Master Yoda about the Force.

[Han], [Leia], en jan ante li tawa ma mun [Cloud City], taso jan lawa [Lando] li pakala tan wile pi [Vader].
Han, Leia, and their companions go to Cloud City, but Governor Lando is pressured into betraying them by Vader.

[Han Solo] li kama kiwen lon sin pi moli [carbonite], en [Leia] li toki e olin suli tawa ona.
Han Solo is frozen in carbonite, and Leia declares her deep love for him.

[Luke] li weka e kama sona pi [Yoda] lon tenpo la li tawa utala tawa [Vader] en li kama sona e ni: jan ma ona li sama jan ike ni.
Luke cuts his training short, confronts Vader, and learns that the villain is his own father.

[Leia], [Lando], en kulupu ante li pana e wile pi alasa e [Han] kepeken tenpo awen.
Leia, Lando, and the other allies promise to search for Han at the first chance.

tenpo lili poka la kulupu ni li pali e awen pi jan [Han] lon ma seli [Tatooine] kepeken wile pi jan lawa kiwen [Jabba].
Soon after, the group executes a mission to rescue Han on hot Tatooine against the crime lord Jabba the Hutt.

[Leia] li weka e [Han], [Luke] li utala e soweli monsuta, en ma ko [barge] pi [Jabba] li pakala lon seli.
Leia frees Han, Luke defeats the desert beasts, and Jabba's sail barge explodes in fire.

ma lawa [Empire] li pali e ilo [Death Star] sin, en kulupu [Rebel] li wile moli e ona kepeken pakala pi awen mun lon ma kasi [Endor].
The Empire builds a second Death Star, and the Rebels aim to destroy it by disabling the shield from the forest moon Endor.

[Han] li lawa e utala lon ma kasi poka pi soweli [Ewok], lon tenpo sama la [Leia] li kama jan pona pi kulupu ni.
Han leads the ground strike alongside the Ewoks, while Leia befriends the forest tribe.

[Luke] li tawa lawa pi [Vader] en [Emperor Palpatine] tawa open e pilin pona lon jan mama ona.
Luke faces Vader and Emperor Palpatine in hopes of awakening the good within his father.

[Emperor] li wile e [Luke] tawa pimeja, taso [Vader] li weka e lawa ike en awen e jan lili ona.
The Emperor tries to pull Luke into darkness, but Vader turns against evil and saves his son.

[Emperor] li moli tan wawa pi [Vader] en [Luke], taso [Vader] li pakala ike en li moli lon poka pi jan lili ona.
The Emperor dies through the combined effort of Vader and Luke, though Vader is mortally wounded and passes away beside his child.

ma kasi [Endor] la soweli [Ewok] en jan [Rebel] li musi tan ni: ma lawa ike li pakala en [Jedi] li awen lon suno.
On Endor the Ewoks and Rebels celebrate because the tyrannical Empire has fallen and the Jedi endure in the light.

lipu ni la utala pi ma [Star Wars] li pini lon wawa sin, lon pilin kulupu, en lon awen pi nasin [Jedi].
Thus the original Star Wars trilogy concludes with renewed hope, community spirit, and the survival of the Jedi path.

# Plot summary / retelling of "The Alchemist" (1908) by H. P. Lovecraft.

jan lawa [Antoine de C] li awen taso lon tomo suli pi kulupu ona lon ma kasi pakala, la kulupu ona li jo ala e mani anu jan pona tan ni: jan ale pi kulupu li moli lon sike [32].
Count Antoine de C. lives in isolation amid the ruined family castle, the last impoverished heir of a line whose sons all perish at the age of thirty-two.

lawa mute pi kon la lipu open lon tomo ona li toki e ni: jan mama pi mama ona li utala e jan wawa [Charles le Sorcier], suli anpa mute, la jan ni li pana e utala kon pi moli sin tawa kulupu [C].
He pores over brittle chronicles explaining how his distant ancestor slew the sorcerer Charles le Sorcier, who cursed every future de C to suffer violent deaths.

tenpo ale la lukin ike li kama: jan ante li kama pakala kepeken selo poki, kepeken seli, kepeken utala; tan ni la jan lawa li pali lon anpa, li ante e ilo pi ko anpa, li awen kute e kalama lili lon tomo moli.
From childhood he has witnessed relatives killed by bizarre accidents, so he studies alchemy, rebuilds passages beneath the castle, and keeps watch over the ominous catacombs.

lon tenpo pimeja wan la kalama wawa li kama tan anpa, la ona li tawa tomo moli pi mama mute, li kama lon lupa sin li lukin e mun walo lon sinpin kiwen.
One night strange noises echo from below; he descends through the ancestral vaults, discovering a hidden chamber lit by ghostly phosphorescence.

lon sina ni la jan kiwen walo li mute e kalama: jan [Charles], taso ona li lukin sama jan pi sike sin tawa ala tan ni: ona li lon ala moli, ona li jo e lawa pi telo wawa lon walo mute.
There he confronts Charles himself—still alive, unnaturally youthful thanks to secret elixirs, and gloating about decades of patient vengeance.

jan lawa li utala e ona kepeken ilo mun, li pakala e jan ike li kute e toki pini: "mi utala tan ni: jan mama sina li seli e mama mi"; tan sina la moli pi kulupu li pini, taso kulupu [C] li moli weka tan jan sin.
Antoine strikes him down with a blade, hearing the sorcerer confess that he survived to avenge his burned parents; with Charles dead, the curse breaks even though the count remains the last of his family.

# Plot summary / retelling of "The Tomb" (1917) by H. P. Lovecraft.

jan [Jervas Dudley] li awen lon sewi pi tomo moli [Hyde], li lape e tenpo pimeja lon poka kiwen ni kepeken pilin wile tawa insa.
Jervas Dudley haunts the exterior of the Hyde family vault, spending his nights beside the stone door yearning to enter.

lon lape ona la, ona li kama jo e kiwen open, li tawa insa en li sama e utala musi pi jan lawa [Jervas Hyde] lon tomo suli pi tenpo pini.
In his dreams he gains a key, walks inside, and joins the decadent revels of the long-dead Jervas Hyde.

tenpo pini la jan ante li alasa e ona lon anpa tomo moli li lukin e seli lon tomo pi kulupu, taso tomo moli li awen pini, tan ni la jan ale li toki e ni: jan [Jervas] taso li pakala lon pilin.
Eventually neighbors find him collapsed by the vault while the Hyde mansion burns elsewhere, and because the crypt is still sealed they call his visions madness.

ona li toki e jan [Samuel Johnson] en kulupu pi lipu suli lon ma [London], li pana e sona musi pi utala uta, pi lipu soweli, en pi mi mute suli.
He brags about meeting Samuel Johnson and the Literary Club in London, sharing playful gossip about debates, plays, and great men.

The rambling style lovingly mocks Lovecraft’s own antiquarian affectations by pretending to be a relic from another age.

# Plot summary / retelling of "The White Ship" (1919) by H. P. Lovecraft.

jan lawa pi suno sewi [Basil Elton] li awen lon noka pi ilo suno lili lon ma [Kingsport], li lukin e telo waso lon tenpo pimeja ale.
Basil Elton, keeper of a small lighthouse near Kingsport, keeps vigil over the wandering night seas.

tenpo mun la waso suli walo li kama lon poka, li lawa e ilo tomo pi nimi [White Ship], en jan sinpin pi linja loje li alasa e luka [Basil].
One moonlit night a great white bird appears, guiding the vessel called the White Ship while a red-bearded helmsman reaches for Basil’s hand.

ona li kama tawa poka, li kalama ala, li toki taso e ni: o tawa insa ilo ni la sina ken lukin e ma sin pi nimi nasa.
The stranger silently invites him aboard and promises the voyage will uncover strange hidden realms.

ilo [White Ship] li tawa ma [Zar], ma pi pilin pi sona weka, taso jan sinpin li toki e ni: jan pi pilin wawa taso li ken awen lon ona.
The White Ship glides past Zar, land of forgotten dreams, while the pilot warns that only steadfast spirits may remain there.

ona li lukin e suno pimeja lon ma [Thalarion], tomo pi soweli monsuta lon telo, en jan sinpin li toki sin e ni: ma ni li moku e jan ale li kama lon ona.
They glimpse the dark spires of Thalarion, a city of monstrous beasts beside the sea, and the pilot repeats that every visitor is devoured.

tenpo kama la ona li tawa ma [Oonai], ma musi pi kalama pi kon sewi, la [Basil] li kute e kalama pi jan lili en li pilin e kon pona sin.
Later they reach Oonai, a joyful land of airy music, where Basil hears children’s songs and feels a fresh serenity.

pini la ona li kama ma [Sona-Nyl], ma pi tenpo ala, pi telo seli pona, li pana e awen lili tawa jan ale.
At last they arrive at Sona-Nyl, the timeless country of warm seas that grants unending rest to all inhabitants.

[Basil] li awen lon ma ni lon sike mute, li kama jan lawa pi tomo kule laso, li awen pilin pona tawa pilin en sijelo.
Basil remains there through many cycles, becomes a gentle steward over blue-domed halls, and enjoys harmony in mind and body.

taso tenpo wan la ona li lukin e suno loje lon anpa mun en li kute e toki lili tan jan sinpin kepeken kon taso: ma pi nimi [Cathuria] li jo e wawa pi wile sona.
One day he notices a red glow beneath the horizon and hears the helmsman whisper that the land named Cathuria holds the ultimate fulfillment of desire.

jan sinpin li toki e ni: o awen lon [Sona-Nyl] tan ni: ma [Cathuria] li wile e jan pi sona ala, taso [Basil] li kute ala e ona li wile alasa.
The pilot begs him to stay in Sona-Nyl because Cathuria destroys all mortals, yet Basil ignores the advice and demands the quest continue.

ilo pi [White Ship] li tawa lon poka suno loje, li anpa lon lupa pimeja pi anpa sewi, la telo suli li weka tan noka ona li kama lon kon pimeja.
The White Ship steers toward the red light, plunges over a black abyss at the world’s rim, and the surrounding waters fall into mist.

jan [Basil] li lon ala ilo ni, li lukin ala e ma [Sona-Nyl], taso li ken ala e ijo sin taso supa pakala pi ilo suno lon ma [Kingsport], en pilin ike pi wile suli taso li awen lon ona.
Basil awakens with no ship or Sona-Nyl in sight, only the shattered parapet of his Kingsport lighthouse and the aching memory of his unreachable longing.

# Plot summary / retelling of "The Doom that Came to Sarnath" (1919) by H. P. Lovecraft.

tenpo pini la jan anpa tan ma lete li kama lon ma lete pi nimi [Mnar] li lukin e ma lete [Ib], ma pi jan len laso sin, pi len telo suli.
In ancient times desert wanderers reached the cold plain of Mnar and saw the swamp city Ib, whose jelly-folk glistened green under wet robes.

jan anpa ni li pilin e ni: ma ni li wawa kin taso li nasa lukin, la ona li pali e tomo suli pi nimi [Sarnath] lon poka telo suli.
They resented that such eerie beings possessed the shore, so they built Sarnath beside the lake to show human splendor.

tenpo kama la jan utala pi [Sarnath] li moli e jan [Ib], li tawa ma len, li pakala e tomo, li lanpan e kiwen pi ijo sewi nimi [Bokrug] tan tomo suli pi jan len.
Later Sarnath’s warriors massacred the Ibians, sacked their wet streets, and stole the stone idol of their lizard god Bokrug.

kiwen ni li lon tomo lawa lon noka telo, ona li kama sitelen pi wawa suli pi jan lawa pi [Sarnath], taso jan sona pi lipu laso li wile e ni: jan mute li o pilin ala.
The idol was set in the great hall as a trophy, yet priests muttered that its glare would bring misfortune.

tenpo mute la ma [Sarnath] li kama suli: tomo sin pi kiwen walo li tawa sewi, mani mute li kama tan kulupu ante, en jan lawa li pali musi pi tenpo [Thraa], [Ilarnak], en [Kadatheron].
Across centuries Sarnath prospered with marble towers, tribute from allied cities, and festivals honoring kings like Thraa, Ilarnak, and Kadatheron.

lon sike pi nanpa mute la jan lawa [Sarnath] li kama kulupu lon tomo suli tawa musi pi tenpo sike nanpa [1000], li wile sewi e utala pi moli seme pi jan len.
At the thousandth anniversary the nobles gathered to celebrate the old victory over the Ibians.

lon tenpo sama la telo suli li kama loje pimeja, waso ike lon kon li kalama seme sin, en kiwen [Bokrug] li weka tan supa.
That night the lake turned blackish-red, strange birds screamed, and the idol vanished from its pedestal.

jan mute li pilin seli wawa li tawa lawa, taso suli pi tomo ni li pakala lon anpa walo: sina ken lukin taso e anpa telo, sijelo ala li lon.
The celebrants panicked, but at dawn the palace lay in ruins besides the lake with no bodies left to find.

jan ante lon ma [Mnar] li kute e kalama sin tan kin pi telo, li lukin e ma ni: tomo ale pi [Sarnath] li lon telo, soweli telo suli li awen lon sewi taso.
Travelers later seen only a glassy lake covering the site, as if Sarnath had been melted beneath the water while reptilian ripples laughed overhead.

tenpo pini la toki tan ma ona li awen taso tan lipu pi jan pi ma len lon noka li toki e ni: jan [Ib] li kama sin lon tenpo mun lon ma lete ante.
The only remembrance came from desert legends claiming the jelly folk revived each moonrise within distant marshes.
