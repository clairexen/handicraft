# A small toki pona <-> english corpus. Lines with '#' as first char are ignored.
# Articles are separated by a '----' line. Within an article each sentence
# is split in two lines. The first line is a toki pona sentence, and the second
# line is its english translation. Sentences are seperated by a blank line.
# English vocabulary can be used within the toki pona sentences by enclosing
# them is square brakets, and vice versa. (this file is 7-bit ASCII only!)

ni li nasin pona lili:
This is a simple example:

toki pona la, ante toki pi [hungry] li ni: (wile moku).
In toki pona, the translation of "hungry" is "want to eat".

[bigotry] li ni:
[bigotry] is this:

jan li lukin ike e jan ante tan ni: jan ante li sama ala ona.
A person judges other people because those people are not the same as them.

ona li wile ala sona e jan ante.
They do not want to know other people.

ona li wile ala kama sona tan jan ante.
They do not want to learn from other people.

tan ni la ona li pana e pilin ike tawa jan ante.
Because of this, they give bad feelings to other people.

ni li pona ala. ni li pakala.
This is not good. This is harmful.

ni ale li [bigotry].
All of this is [bigotry].

----

[flying monkey] li jan pona lukin pi jan ike, taso ona li pali e wile pi jan ike.
A [flying monkey] is someone who looks like a friend of the abusive person, yet carries out that person's wishes.

[narzisstic abuse] la jan ike li wile lawa e ale, li wile ala kute e pilin ante.
In [narzisstic abuse], the harmful person wants to control everything and does not want to hear other feelings.

jan ike li toki wawa tawa [flying monkey] kepeken musi, pi mi mute, anu pakala.
The abuser speaks strongly to the [flying monkey] using flattery, shared secrets, or threats.

tan ni la [flying monkey] li lon lawa pi jan ike, li pali sama waso lete lon sewi.
Because of this the [flying monkey] moves under the abuser's control and strikes from a distance.

ona li toki e ijo ike tawa jan ante, li pali e pakala tawa nimi pi jan ike.
They spread harmful messages to other people and carry out damage in the abuser's name.

jan ante li pilin pakala tan ni la kulupu li pakala kepeken wawa sin.
Other people feel hurt, so the community breaks further with new force.

jan sona li ken weka e wile pi [flying monkey] kepeken sona, nasin lawa, en pilin pona pi jan ante.
Knowledgeable helpers can remove the [flying monkey]'s mission through education, boundaries, and empathy for others.

ni ale li [flying monkey] lon ma pi [narzisstic abuse].
All of this is a [flying monkey] within the world of [narzisstic abuse].
