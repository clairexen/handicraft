## Non-token embeddings

(the following names for non-token embeddings are used for
documentation, comments, and log messages. the syntax looks
similar to the syntax we are going to use for our special
token, but use [] instead of <> to distinguish them from the
embedding names that correspond to special tokens below.

[|pos:0|] [|pos:1|] ... [|pos:BS-1|]
  position embeddings for the max BS positions in a sequence

[|think:1/2|] [|think:2/2|]
  additional "think" embeddings for think2x mode, in think2x
  mode we only use BS/2 position embeddings and input tokens,
  double each of the BS/2 input token+position encodings so
  we are back to BS elements in our block, and then add
  [|think:1/2|] to the even and [|think:2/2|] to the odd
  positions.

[|think:1/4|] [|think:2/4|] [|think:3/4|] [|think:4/4|]
  additional "think" embeddings for think4x mode

<|----|>

<|tokipona:> <|english:>

</english:> </tokipona:>
